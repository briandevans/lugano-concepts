Privacy Policy

Private AI products store nothing. We verify everything.

Lugano is built for private AI work where privacy is enforced by architecture, not explained after the fact.

Prompts and outputs

The product is designed so sensitive inference data stays inside the verified path, not in a Lugano training corpus.

Ceresio website assistant

Ceresio is a website assistant separate from Lugano's private AI inference offering. Conversations and any contact details you choose to share are stored in an isolated database to operate the assistant and enable team review.

Messages are processed by model providers through Wafer and OpenRouter. Do not submit confidential or sensitive information.
