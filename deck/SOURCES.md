## Slide 02 — Cisco figures

Cisco 2024 Data Privacy Benchmark Study (2,600 privacy and security professionals, 12 geographies), announced 25 Jan 2024:

- 27% banned GenAI applications, at least temporarily
- 61% limit which GenAI tools employees may use
- 63% limit what data may be entered

Primary: [Cisco newsroom](https://newsroom.cisco.com/c/r/newsroom/en/us/a/y2024/m01/organizations-ban-use-of-generative-ai-over-data-privacy-security-cisco-study.html) and [study PDF](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en_us/about/doing_business/trust-center/docs/cisco-privacy-benchmark-study-2024.pdf).

## Slide 03 — Trust timeline

- **Five-year retention / training:** Anthropic consumer terms update (2025). If a user allows model improvement, new or resumed chats and coding sessions may be retained up to five years. [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/updates-to-our-consumer-terms).
- **Court preservation:** S.D.N.Y. Magistrate Judge Ona T. Wang, 13 May 2025, directed OpenAI to preserve and segregate output log data that would otherwise be deleted, including on user deletion. *The New York Times Co. v. Microsoft Corp.*, 1:23-cv-11195.
- **Indexed chats:** 404 Media / The Verge, Aug 2025 — a researcher scraped nearly 100,000 shared ChatGPT conversations that Google had indexed.
- **Petition:** Change.org petition to keep GPT-4o; Business Insider (17 Feb 2026) reported roughly 21,900 signatures. OpenAI retired GPT-4o from ChatGPT on 13 Feb 2026.
